## Summary

An openly-licensed, well-documented database of crop varieties and their traits, compiled from open gene-bank and catalog sources with per-entry provenance.

## Scope

In scope: documented variety/trait records from open sources with citations. Out of scope: proprietary catalogs, or agronomic dosing/prescription advice.

## Risk

Medium: built on openly-licensed sources with recorded provenance; educational/derivative in nature, no personal data, and no farm-/case-specific prescriptive advice. Each deliverable must cite sources and stay within established, verifiable material.

## Approach

Each task defines the schema or adds a documented set of variety records with sources and licenses.

## Definition of shipped

A documented, openly-licensed database of crop varieties and traits with per-entry provenance, published openly and used by an agricultural org.
